# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

BlinkLane welcomes respectful collaboration.

欢迎用尊重、清晰、负责的方式参与 BlinkLane。

## Expected

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, but ask for clarification when needed.
- Protect privacy when discussing dashcam material.
- Keep legal and enforcement claims careful and evidence-based.

## Not Accepted

- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Posting private dashcam footage, license plates, faces, or GPS traces without consent.
- Requests to bypass CAPTCHA, login controls, platform rules, or legal safeguards.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these expectations.
